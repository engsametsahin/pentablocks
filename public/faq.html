PentaBlocks FAQ

Is PentaBlocks free to play?

Yes. The core browser game is free. Optional monetization features may be introduced later.

Is this a Katamino game?

PentaBlocks is an original browser puzzle inspired by Katamino style piece-fitting gameplay.

Do puzzles always have a valid solution?

Yes. Puzzle generation is constrained by solvability checks and curated pools.

How does multiplayer stay fair?

Both players receive the same board and piece set with synchronized countdown and shared deadline logic.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Mobile supports drag controls, one-tap rotate, and hold-to-flip interaction patterns.

Do guest stats and account stats mix?

No. Guest and authenticated progress are handled separately to protect account-based records.

What is Arena mode?

Arena is ranked queue play with rating tiers and structured matchmaking. Read the Arena Guide.

Where can I learn strategy?

Start with Strategy Basics, then continue to Advanced Tips.
