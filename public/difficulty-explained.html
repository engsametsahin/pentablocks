PentaBlocks Difficulty System

PentaBlocks is tuned around progressive challenge, not random spikes. As levels increase, board geometry, piece variety, and timer pressure evolve together. This keeps each new band meaningful while still solvable.

Difficulty Bands

Band Level Range Focus
Easy 1-10 Core controls, simple fit recognition, generous timers
Moderate 11-30 Path planning, avoiding dead-end cavities
Hard 31-60 Branch control and faster correction
Very Hard 61-90 Dense layouts, strict timers, advanced shape ordering
Extreme 91-100 Top-end speed and precision under pressure

What Changes Behind the Scenes

Difficulty is affected by board dimensions, piece count, shape composition, and target timer. Higher levels prefer piece sets that produce tighter constraints and fewer trivial openings. The system avoids unsolvable sets and validates puzzle integrity before use.

Why This Matters for Players

You can predict the kind of skill each range tests. If you plateau, practice one band below your current limit and then return. This is usually better than grinding only the hardest board you unlocked.
