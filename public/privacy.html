PentaBlocks Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-16

What We Store

PentaBlocks stores gameplay data in your browser using local storage. This can include completed levels, best times, and local player stats.

Analytics

PentaBlocks may collect basic analytics events such as session start, level start, level completion, game mode activity, and error diagnostics. These events help us improve gameplay quality, balance, and reliability across desktop and mobile devices.

Advertising

If ads are enabled in future releases, ad display pacing is limited and intended to avoid disrupting onboarding. Additional ad controls may depend on your consent selection.

Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains in your browser unless you clear browser storage. Future account-based features may introduce cloud storage for scores and progression.

Account Data

When you create an account (guest, nickname, Google, or email-based login), we may store profile details, progression, best times, multiplayer stats, and session tokens needed for authentication and continuity.

Security

We use secure transport (HTTPS) and server-side controls to protect account and gameplay data. No internet service can be guaranteed 100 percent secure, but we continuously improve safeguards.

Contact

For privacy or policy questions, contact: legal@pentablocks.live
