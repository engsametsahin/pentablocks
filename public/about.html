About PentaBlocks

PentaBlocks is a web-first puzzle game created by TGS LABS. The goal is to deliver fast, fair, and skill-based geometric puzzle play for both casual and competitive players.

Design Philosophy

We focus on clarity and depth: simple controls, meaningful progression, and puzzles that reward reasoning. Every level should feel solvable, readable, and worth replaying for better time and star ratings.

Product Direction

Current priorities include stable multiplayer, ranked Arena balancing, mobile optimization, and account quality. Future milestones include deeper social features, additional competitive formats, and native app rollout.

Play and Learn

Start the game at pentablocks.live and explore guides: How to Play, Strategy Basics, and Arena Guide.
